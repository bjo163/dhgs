# DHGS Content Style

## Voice

Calm, precise, respectful, non-accusatory, and explicit about uncertainty.

## Rules

1. Explain what happened before explaining internal terminology.
2. Explain consequences and next steps.
3. Never hide appeal, correction, or deadlines in footnotes.
4. Distinguish user-correctable errors from platform failures.
5. Do not imply guilt before a lawful determination.
6. Use `unresolved` when evidence does not justify certainty.
7. Public summaries should support Simple, Standard, and Technical depth.

## Examples

**Technical:** `Jurisdiction invalid.`  
**Simple:** `This office does not have authority to decide this case.`

**Technical:** `Evidence threshold not met.`  
**Simple:** `There is not enough verified information to make this decision yet.`

**Avoid:** `You failed to complete the process.`  
**Prefer when true:** `The system still needs the following information before review can continue.`
