# DHGS Corpus

Corpus is curated reusable knowledge. It is **not case evidence** and does not independently create coercive authority.

Initial domains:

- `legal/` — laws and legal references after a jurisdiction is selected and qualified review exists.
- `rights/` — rights frameworks and safeguards.
- `science/` — scientific references with methodology/provenance.
- `governance/` — DHGS governance references.
- `ethical/` — ethical principles and commentary.
- `scriptural/` — primary/scriptural references and clearly separated interpretations.
- `policy/` — institutional policy.
- `procedure/` — operational procedure.
- `precedent/` — applicable precedent or prior decisions.
- `lessons/` — reviewed lessons learned.

All authoritative corpus artifacts require provenance, version, status and review context.
